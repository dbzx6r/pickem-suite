# Player guide

Get your server address and account from your commissioner. There is no central Pickem Suite account or hosted service.

1. Sign in and change a temporary password when prompted.
2. Open **Picks**, choose the week, and select a team for every available game. Check that your selections saved. Picks lock individually at kickoff; postponements and previously locked games may remain protected.
3. Enter the predicted combined score for the designated tiebreaker game before it locks.
4. Use **Standings** for weekly and overall season ranks. A live lead is provisional; finalized scores determine the official result. The weekly champion is highlighted.
5. Open a game for available quarter scores and live detail. Other players’ choices become visible after that game starts, not while it is still open for picks.
6. Visit your profile for statistics, records, achievements, favorite-team colors, and theme preferences, including OLED black.
7. Use league chat for conversation and stake-free challenges for bragging rights. No payment, prize, or valuable stake is permitted.

The website works on phones and can be added to the home screen. Notification support depends on the browser/OS and permission settings. Enable reminders where supported, and allow notifications only on devices you want notified. The server checks missing picks, but an already-sent alert may arrive after you submit. Always confirm your picks yourself.

This repository does not distribute a native app. If your commissioner supplies a compatible native client, use that server’s HTTPS origin and credentials; APNs requires the operator’s own configuration.

Ask the commissioner for account recovery or deletion. Do not share passwords, API keys, private league screenshots, or notification tokens in public support issues.
