## External sports/news data

The optional ESPN adapter accesses third-party interfaces for schedules, results, odds, and game detail. This project does not supply a data license, an API service agreement, or an availability guarantee. Public reachability is not permission for automated collection, republication, or commercial exploitation. Review applicable [Disney/ESPN terms](https://disneytermsofuse.com/english/) and any supplemental conditions before enabling it. Respect restrictions and rate limits; obtain permission or use your own licensed provider where necessary.

External data requests are off by default. News also has a commissioner setting. No scraped news archive, sports-data dump, or official media library is distributed. Scores and odds may be delayed, missing, or corrected; they are informational, not betting advice. No game video is supplied.

## No gambling, stakes, or prizes

Use this suite for free, private predictions and bragging rights only. Do not charge entry fees, collect or settle bets, award cash or valuable prizes, or arrange off-platform stakes. “Side bet” names in legacy internal routes/models do not authorize wagering: the website rejects stakes and the native challenge contract is stake-free. The code provides no payments or payouts.

For example, [Washington RCW 9.46.0237](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=9.46.0237) defines gambling in terms of risking something of value on specified uncertain outcomes for something of value. Goods and services can raise issues too; restricting a feature to “lunch” is not a general legal exemption. Laws differ by state and circumstances. This repository is not a fifty-state gambling-law clearance, and a disclaimer cannot legalize an otherwise prohibited activity.

## Privacy and operator responsibilities

Operators choose who can join and must safeguard account identifiers, credentials, picks, chat, avatars, and notification subscriptions. Explain what you store and which external services receive requests. Logo requests may expose network information to a CDN; sports requests go to the provider; enabled push uses browser push services or Apple. The project has no central account system, telemetry collector, or hosted participant database. Hosting providers and configured third parties have their own practices.

Keep registration closed, collect only necessary information, honor applicable privacy obligations, and handle account/retention requests. Do not assume this project includes legal terms, privacy compliance, age verification, or licensing for your deployment. No warranty or promise of legal compliance is provided.
